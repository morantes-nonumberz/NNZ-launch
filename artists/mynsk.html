About

WHO IS
MYNSK?

MYNSK operates in the space between urban and alternative — genre-fluid by design, and distinctive by instinct. His music carries the weight of hip-hop's storytelling tradition while pushing into sonic territory that few Colombian artists are willing to explore. He doesn't need a category. He needs space, and he uses it well.

The name MYNSK isn't random — it signals something intentional about how this artist sees himself. An outlier. Not defined by geography, not limited by genre convention, not in a hurry to sound like anyone else. His music rewards careful listening because there are layers in it that surface on repeated plays: production details, lyrical references, and a vocal quality that has a character of its own.

His 2024 release esfume is a demonstration of what his music is capable of at its most focused. The title itself — which translates roughly as "vanish" or "fade away" — reflects an artistic sensibility that is comfortable with ambiguity and atmosphere rather than straightforward narrative. It's the kind of music that exists in the feeling more than the story, which is exactly what makes it resonate.

As a 2024 addition to the No Numberz (NNZ) roster, MYNSK brings a unique creative voice to an agency already defined by its commitment to artists who refuse to fit neatly into any single box. Medellín has always been a city of reinvention — MYNSK is one of the artists defining what its next chapter in music sounds like.

THE
MUSIC

MYNSK releases music at the boundary of Urban, Hip-Hop and Alternative — each track built with intention and care. Available on Spotify and all major platforms.

01
esfume Single · Urban / Alternative · 2024

esfume is MYNSK at his most cinematic. The track is built around atmosphere — a production that expands and contracts, giving the vocals room to move through it rather than sit on top of it. Lyrically, it deals in the feeling of impermanence: things fading, relationships dissolving, moments slipping. It's an ambitious track that demonstrates exactly why MYNSK is a different kind of artist within the NNZ roster.

Atmospheric Hip-Hop

MYNSK's music lives in the atmosphere around the notes as much as the notes themselves. His productions are layered and deliberately paced — creating a space the listener can actually inhabit rather than just observe from the outside.

Alternative Sensibility

What separates MYNSK from the rest of the urban music scene in Colombia is his willingness to sit in ambiguity. His music doesn't always resolve in the expected direction — and that tension is what makes it genuinely interesting to return to.

Medellín's New Wave

Colombian hip-hop's alternative wing is still being defined, and MYNSK is one of the artists doing the defining. His music represents what Medellín's creative scene looks like when it stops looking backward for reference and starts building something entirely its own.
