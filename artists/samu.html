About

WHO IS
SAMU?

SAMU moves where genres blur. His music pulls from Colombian urban roots, the emotional architecture of trap, and the restless experimentation of alternative — producing a sound that resists easy categorization and hits harder because of it. He doesn't chase a lane. He carves one.

Based in Medellín and operating at the intersection of urban music and alternative production, SAMU brings a different kind of creative energy to the NNZ roster. Where some artists find their voice by narrowing their focus, SAMU's strength is in synthesis — he has listened widely, absorbed deeply, and built a sound that reflects all of it without being defined by any single one.

His 2024 single MINION (Prod. Jpssnd) showcases the range of what his music can be: a track built on unexpected production choices that somehow land exactly right. The collaboration with Jpssnd signals the kind of artistic partnerships SAMU gravitates toward — producers who push him rather than settle for what's safe or familiar.

Joining No Numberz (NNZ) in 2024, SAMU arrived with a clear artistic point of view: music that feels personal but connects broadly. His approach to each release reflects a commitment to craft over convenience — he would rather take longer to get something right than release something that doesn't represent where he actually is as an artist.
