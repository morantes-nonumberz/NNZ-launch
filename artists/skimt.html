About

WHO IS
SKIMT?

SkiMT is a Colombian artist from Medellín whose music refuses to sit inside a single genre. Since his earliest releases he has moved fluidly between R&B's melodic depth, trap's kinetic energy, and reggaeton's rhythmic pull — building a sound that feels distinctly Colombian while drawing from a wider global palette.

Raised in a city where music has always been a language of ambition and identity, SkiMT developed his ear for production and phrasing organically. His voice carries weight without force — measured, intentional, and confident in a way that only comes from someone who has spent real time studying what makes a song connect with people.

In 2024, SkiMT joined the No Numberz (NNZ) roster alongside Rico TBW, SAMU and MYNSK. With professional management behind him, his focus shifted from just making music to building a career with structure and long-term vision. Releases like SUEÑOX and HECHIZZO ft. YEDI & JPSSND began landing on Spotify listeners' radars, demonstrating both his versatility as an artist and his consistency as a creator.

His influences span from the emotional rawness of American R&B to the street-level storytelling of Latin trap — but what makes SkiMT stand out is the synthesis. He doesn't sound like a copy of any single reference. He sounds like Medellín in 2024: global in taste, local in feeling, and urgent in energy.

For bookings, collaborations, or press — contact NNZ management at morantes@nonumberz.com.

Sound

THE
SOUND

♪

Melodic Trap

SkiMT brings melody to environments built for hard percussion. His hooks land with R&B-level precision but carry the production weight of Colombian trap — a combination that defines his signature sound and sets him apart from his peers.

♪

R&B Depth

Emotional depth is central to SkiMT's writing. His lyrical approach borrows from the storytelling tradition of American R&B — direct, personal, and built around a mood rather than just a topic. He writes songs that land differently depending on where you are in your life.

♪

Colombian Identity

Born and raised in Medellín, SkiMT's music carries the rhythmic and cultural DNA of a city that has always produced artists who blend international sounds with a very specific local perspective. That tension is what makes his music feel both familiar and genuinely fresh.
